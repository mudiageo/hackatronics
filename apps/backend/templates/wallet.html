Why this matters for the evidence model

Today a counter sale is typed in, so it sits at SELF-REPORTED. With the wallet collecting the payment, the same sale settles through the rail and moves up to SETTLED automatically — no extra work for the pharmacist. Deposits stay with Wema, and the lending book gets a repayment stream it can see.
