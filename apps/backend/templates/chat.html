AI business advisor V2

Ask questions about your own numbers — grounded in your verified ledger, not in general advice.

What it will answer

  • "Why did my readiness score drop this month?"
  • "Which products are moving slowest?"
  • "How much more attested revenue do I need for a ₦2m limit?"

How it stays honest

The backend filters the business's own data before anything reaches the model — the same authorisation rules that refuse a bank access to raw books apply here. The assistant can only discuss what the asking role is allowed to see, and every answer cites the records behind it.
