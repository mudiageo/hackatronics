How it will work

  1. Pharmacist says: "sold two packs of Amoxicillin for three thousand naira, cash"
  2. Speech is transcribed, then parsed the same way a scanned receipt is
  3. Products are matched against real inventory — the model never picks an ID
  4. A confirmation card appears; nothing saves until the human confirms

Evidence tier

A spoken sale records at SELF-REPORTED, exactly like typing it. Voice removes the keyboard, not the need for independent evidence. Only a prescription redeemed at a different organisation reaches ATTESTED.
